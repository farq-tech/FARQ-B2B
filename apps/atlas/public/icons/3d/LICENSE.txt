Farq Atlas 3D icon assets
=========================

The PNG icons in this directory are custom assets prepared for Farq Atlas and may be used by Farq commercially at no charge. They contain no third-party marks.

The new 3D illustrations were generated using the FLUX.1 [schnell] model. The model is released under Apache License 2.0; its model licence does not itself define rights in any particular generated output. Model information: https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell

Microsoft Fluent Emoji legacy attribution
